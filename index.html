Welcome to the Islands of Taniti

White sand beaches, lush rainforest, and a glowing volcano set in the tropical Pacific. While the island has an area of less than 500 square miles, the terrain is varied and includes both sandy and rocky beaches, a small but safe harbor, lush tropical rainforests, and a mountainous interior that includes a small, active volcano. Taniti has an indigenous population of about 20,000.

Check out what Taniti has to offer. We can't wait to see you!

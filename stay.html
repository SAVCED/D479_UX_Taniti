Hotels

Taniti offers a large selection of top tier hotels. We pride ourselves in service and comfortability. Many of our hotels have great views of the ocean while also being close to the city and the rainforests.

Now featuring comfortable rooms in Taniti City and along the coast, with private bathrooms, air conditioning, and easy access to restaurants and transport.

Beaches

Taniti's coastline offers calm lagoons for swimming, reefs for snorkeling, and long stretches of white sand perfect for sunset walks.

Enjoy snorkeling, swimming, surfing, and parasailing along our coastlines.

Join our expert fishermen on a chartered fishing tour!

Rainforest

Guided trails wind through dense tropical canopy past waterfalls, wild orchids, and native birds found nowhere else in the Pacific.

For the thrill seekers, try out our ziplines through the rainforest!

Taniti City

The island's capital features open-air markets, museums, live music venues, and a lively waterfront promenade.

There is fun for the whole family with our arcades, bowling alley, and movie theater.

Adults may enjoy our numerous pubs, including a microbrewery and our nightclub featuring the islands best musicians.

Food and Drink

Enjoy fresh-caught seafood, tropical fruit, and local specialties at beachside grills, family restaurants, and street food stalls.

You don't want to miss our very own Michellin star restaurant, Pisce de Romano! Located within our Sunset Villas Resort.
